Applications

Software capabilities and the instances that implement them. Each application groups instances across devices (a systemd unit, a PM2 app, a container, or a plain process); every instance keeps independent state, and the application's health is a rollup of its instances escalated by its criticality -- so a stopped critical service degrades its instance, and a healthy sibling never masks a critical failing one. Checks run through registered strategies on their own poll cadence; a failed check never erases the last success, it only ages the state.
